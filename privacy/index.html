Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 7, 2026

VisaGo ("we," "our," or "the app") is a travel visa requirements and trip planning application. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

The short version: Your passport info, trips, documents, and personal data stay on your device. We don't sell your data. We don't show ads. We collect minimal analytics to improve the app.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Passport information — Your selected nationality, passport expiry dates, and multi-passport configurations
  • Trip data — Saved destinations, travel dates, and trip checklist progress
  • Documents — Photos and scans of travel documents (passports, visas, insurance, flight bookings) stored in the Document Holder
  • Visa stay records — Active visa stays and Schengen calculator entries
  • Currency preferences — Your selected currencies and conversion history
  • App preferences — Language selection, notification settings, and display preferences

This data exists only on your device. If you uninstall the app, this data is permanently deleted. We cannot access, view, or recover this data.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Analytics Data

We collect anonymous usage analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve it. This includes:

  • Which screens and features are used most frequently
  • App crashes and error reports
  • General device information (OS version, screen size, device model)
  • Country-level location (derived from your IP address, not GPS)
  • App version and language setting

Analytics data is anonymous and cannot be used to identify you personally. We use Google Firebase Analytics for this purpose.

2.2 Subscription Data

If you purchase a premium subscription, the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play. We receive:

  • Confirmation that a subscription is active
  • The subscription plan type (monthly or annual)
  • Subscription status (active, cancelled, expired)

We do not receive or store your payment method, credit card number, or billing address. All payment processing is handled by Google Play in accordance with their privacy policy.

2.3 Referral and Promo Codes

If you enter a referral code during onboarding, we store the code to attribute your download to the referring partner. If you redeem a promo code for premium access, the code is validated against our server and marked as used. We store:

  • The referral code you entered (if any)
  • The promo code you redeemed (if any)
  • The timestamp of redemption

This data is used solely for partner commission tracking and cannot be used to identify you personally.

2.4 Partner Applications

If you apply to the VisaGo Partner Program through our website, we collect the information you provide in the application form: name, email, partner type, platform, audience size, website, and any additional message. This data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure and used exclusively to process your partner application.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

We want to be explicit about what we do not do:

  • No advertising. VisaGo does not display ads of any kind. We do not share data with advertising networks.
  • No data selling. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • No GPS tracking. We do not access your device's GPS or precise location.
  • No contact access. We do not access your contacts, phone calls, or messages.
  • No document transmission. Photos and documents stored in the Document Holder never leave your device.
  • No account system. The app does not require you to create an account with a username and password.

4. Third-Party Services

VisaGo uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — Anonymous app usage analytics. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — Subscription payment processing. Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare — Promo code validation and partner dashboard hosting. Cloudflare Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention

Local data on your device persists until you delete it within the app or uninstall the app. Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months by Firebase Analytics. Subscription records are retained by Google Play according to their retention policies. Partner application data is retained until the application is processed and for a reasonable period afterward.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your local data at any time by clearing app data in your device settings or uninstalling the app
  • Opt out of analytics by disabling usage data sharing in your device settings
  • Request deletion of partner application data by emailing us
  • Cancel your subscription at any time through Google Play

For European Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation, including the right to access, rectification, erasure, and data portability. Since we store minimal data and most data exists only on your device, exercising these rights is straightforward. Contact us for any GDPR-related requests.

For California Users (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

VisaGo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Security

Your local data is protected by your device's built-in security (screen lock, encryption). Data transmitted to our promo code validation server uses HTTPS encryption. We do not store passwords or sensitive authentication credentials on our servers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised accordingly. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us
